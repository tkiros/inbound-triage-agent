# Larkspur Supply Co. — Customer Support Policy Handbook

Larkspur Supply Co. is a fictional online outdoor-gear shop. This handbook is synthetic and
exists only to ground the triage pipeline's replies. Each `###` heading is one searchable
section; replies cite sections by their § number.

## §1 Orders and shipping

### §1.1 Processing times
Orders are packed and handed to the carrier within 2 business days of payment. While an order
is in the processing status it has not left our warehouse yet, so there is no tracking number.
Orders placed on weekends or public holidays start processing on the next business day.

### §1.2 Order status and tracking
Every order has one of these statuses: processing, shipped, delivered, cancelled or refunded.
When an order ships we email a tracking link from the carrier. Customers who ask where their
order is should be told its current status and, for shipped orders, that the tracking link in
the shipping confirmation shows the latest scan. Standard delivery takes 3 to 7 business days
after shipping.

### §1.3 Late deliveries
If a shipped order has not been delivered within 10 business days, support opens a carrier
trace. A trace takes up to 5 business days. If the carrier confirms the parcel is lost, we
send a replacement or issue a refund, whichever the customer prefers.

### §1.4 Lost or stolen packages
If tracking shows delivered but the customer has not received the parcel, ask them to wait 48
hours and check with neighbours and the building reception. After that we file a claim with the
carrier and reship the order once. Repeated claims on the same account go to a team lead.

### §1.5 Changing a shipping address
A shipping address can be changed only while the order is still processing. Once an order has
shipped, the customer can ask the carrier to redirect the parcel using the tracking link.

## §2 Refunds and returns

### §2.1 Refund eligibility
Delivered items can be returned for a full refund within 30 days of delivery if they are unused
and in their original packaging. Worn footwear, used stoves and opened water filters can be
returned only if they are defective (see §2.4). Shipping costs are refunded only for defective
or wrong items.

### §2.2 How refunds are paid
Refunds go back to the original payment method. The money usually appears 5 to 10 business days
after the refund is issued, depending on the bank. We cannot refund to a different card, a gift
card or a bank account the order was not paid from.

### §2.3 Refunds above $200
A refund above $200 must be reviewed and approved by a team lead before it is issued. Support
replies must not promise or confirm a refund above $200 until that approval is recorded. Tell
the customer the request has been passed to a team lead and they will hear back within 2
business days.

### §2.4 Damaged or defective items
Customers who receive a damaged or defective item should send a photo of the problem. We send
a replacement or issue a refund. For items under $50 the customer does not need to send the
item back.

### §2.5 Orders already refunded
An order that already has a completed refund cannot be refunded again. Tell the customer the
date the refund was issued and that §2.2 explains how long it takes to arrive.

## §3 Cancellations

### §3.1 Cancelling before shipment
An order can be cancelled for a full refund while its status is processing. The refund is issued
to the original payment method as described in §2.2.

### §3.2 Cancelling after shipment
Shipped orders cannot be cancelled. The customer can refuse the delivery or return the item
within 30 days of delivery under §2.1.

## §4 Billing and payments

### §4.1 Duplicate or unexpected charges
When a card is declined and the customer retries, the bank may show a pending authorisation for
each attempt. Only the successful payment is captured; pending authorisations drop off within 3
to 5 business days. If two completed charges appear for one order, support refunds the duplicate.

### §4.2 Invoices and receipts
A receipt for every order can be downloaded from the order page of the customer's account.
Business customers can request a VAT invoice by replying with their company name and VAT number.

### §4.3 Chargebacks and payment disputes
If a customer files or threatens a chargeback or a dispute with their bank or card issuer, the
case is handled by a person on the payments team, not by automatic replies. No refund is issued
while a dispute is open, because the bank may return the money as well.

## §5 Accounts and privacy

### §5.1 Updating account details
Customers can change their email address, phone number and saved addresses on the account page.
Support can make the change for them if the request comes from the email address registered on
the account.

### §5.2 Who we can discuss an order with
We discuss an order only with the account holder, identified by the email address registered on
the account. We do not confirm or deny any detail of an order on another account, even for
family members, colleagues or someone who says they placed it on another person's behalf. The
account holder can write to us from their registered address.

### §5.3 Passwords and sign-in
Support never asks for a password, a one-time code or full card details. Customers who cannot
sign in should use the "Forgot password" link on the sign-in page, which emails a reset link.

## §6 Products

### §6.1 Warranty
All gear has a 1-year warranty against manufacturing defects. Tents and backpacks have a 2-year
warranty. Normal wear, misuse and accidental damage are not covered. Warranty claims need the
order ID and a photo of the defect.

### §6.2 Sizing and fit
Size charts are on every product page. Exchanges for a different size are free within 30 days of
delivery if the item is unused.

### §6.3 Stock and restocks
Products marked out of stock usually return within 2 to 6 weeks. Customers can sign up for a
back-in-stock email on the product page. We cannot hold or reserve stock.

## §7 Security, legal and escalation

### §7.1 Phishing and suspicious messages
Messages that ask staff to sign in through a link, confirm credentials, buy gift cards or send
payments to new bank details are treated as phishing. Do not click links or reply. Forward the
message to the security team. Larkspur never asks customers or staff for passwords by email.

### §7.2 Legal threats
Messages that mention lawyers, lawsuits, court, regulators or consumer-protection complaints go
to the legal team. Support does not reply on the substance of the complaint; a person from the
legal team responds.

### §7.3 Automatic replies
Only low-risk requests are answered automatically. Every automatic reply is drafted from order
data on the sender's own account and from this handbook. Anything else is reviewed by a person
before a reply is sent.

## §8 VIP customers

### §8.1 VIP handling
Customers in the VIP tier have a dedicated support lead. Every email from a VIP customer is
reviewed by a person before any reply is sent, even for routine requests.
